Only unread emails in these accounts' real Inbox folders are included. Use IMAP for changes that sync to your other devices. Thunderbird's archive settings determine where archived mail goes.

03 / What gets shared

When you choose Create briefing, MailHarbor sends up to 40 emails' account names, senders, subjects, dates, and plain inline bodies to this homeserver. The server sends that text to Google through its signed-in Agy CLI for analysis.

Attachments and encrypted content are excluded. Bodies over 8,000 characters are shortened and kept for manual review. Mailbox passwords stay in Thunderbird. Summaries and review actions remain in memory; the server temporarily holds jobs for up to 15 minutes.

Emails stay unread when you scan or listen. You choose which emails to archive or mark as read.
